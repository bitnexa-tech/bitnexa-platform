Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before initiating your project with Bitnexa.

Q: How long does it take to build and deploy a website?

Standard business websites and high-converting landing pages are fully designed, coded, and deployed live within 3 to 5 business days. Large-scale e-commerce engines or custom full-stack web applications typically take 7 to 14 days.

Q: Will you connect and launch the website on our custom domain (.com / .in)?

Yes, absolutely! We connect your GoDaddy, Namecheap, Hostinger, or Google domain directly to Cloudflare edge CDN and high-speed Render Cloud servers, complete with free lifetime SSL (HTTPS) encryption and zero downtime handover.

Q: What are the standard payment terms and milestones?

Typically, 40% initial token deposit is required to begin engineering, and the remaining 60% balance is settled after you review and approve the live staging preview. We accept UPI, Bank Transfer, and secure international credit/debit cards.

Q: Do you offer recurring monthly packages for reels and video editing?

Yes! We provide dedicated monthly agency retainer packages delivering 12 to 20 high-retention reels per month (with visual hooks, dynamic kinetic subtitles, and viral sound curation) delivered on strict schedule.
